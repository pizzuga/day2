🦐 Spaghetti allo Scoglio
A classic Italian seafood pasta with clams, mussels, shrimp, and calamari

🍝 Ingredients (2 servings)
200 g spaghetti

300 g mussels, cleaned

300 g clams, purged

150 g shrimp, peeled (leave tails on if you like)

150 g calamari, cleaned and sliced

2 cloves garlic, crushed

6–8 cherry tomatoes, halved

½ glass dry white wine

Extra‑virgin olive oil

Fresh parsley, chopped

Salt

Chili flakes (optional)

🔪 Instructions
1. Prepare the shellfish
Heat a large pan with a drizzle of olive oil and one garlic clove.

Add clams and mussels, pour in the white wine, cover, and cook until they open.

Remove from heat, discard any that stayed closed, and keep the cooking liquid (strain it through a fine sieve).

2. Cook the seafood
In another pan, heat olive oil with the second garlic clove.

Add shrimp and calamari; sauté for 2–3 minutes.

Add cherry tomatoes and cook until they soften.

Pour in the filtered shellfish liquid and simmer gently.

3. Cook the pasta
Boil spaghetti in salted water until al dente.

4. Combine everything
Add mussels and clams (with shells) to the seafood pan.

Transfer the spaghetti directly into the pan and toss over high heat for 1 minute.

Add chopped parsley and chili flakes if desired.

5. Serve
Plate generously with all the seafood on top.

Finish with a drizzle of olive oil.
